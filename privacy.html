Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

The short version: your daily photos stay on your phone. When you run a skin check, that one photo is sent to an AI service to be analyzed and is not stored by us. You don't create an account, and you can delete everything on your phone at any time.

Who we are

Glowstreak ("we", "us") is a skin progress tracking app operated by Austin Nieves. Contact us at auracheck.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is never uploaded to us:

  • Your daily photos (they are not saved to your camera roll unless you choose to save a timelapse video).
  • Your skin check results, streaks, habit logs and quiz answers (skin type, goal, current routine).
  • Your age confirmation and consent choices.

Skin checks and AI analysis

When you choose to run a skin check, the app sends a reduced-size copy of that photo, together with your quiz answers (skin type, main goal, current routine), to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini AI service for analysis. The results are sent back to your phone.

  • We do not store your photo, your quiz answers or your results on our servers.
  • Google processes the photo to produce the analysis under its Gemini API terms for paid services: Google does not use it to train or improve its products, and keeps it only temporarily where needed for abuse monitoring and legal compliance.
  • Skin checks only describe visible skin. They never rate your looks and are not a medical diagnosis.
  • You will be asked for your consent before your first skin check.

What we store on our server

To run the app without accounts, Glowstreak creates an anonymous user ID for your installation using Supabase (our backend provider). Linked to that anonymous ID, our server stores only:

  • A count of your skin checks per day (date and time), used to enforce daily limits.
  • Technical error reports when a skin check fails (an error code and a technical message, never your photo or results), kept so we can fix problems.

The anonymous ID is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID by us.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your anonymous user ID and your purchase and subscription status from the App Store so the app can unlock Pro features. We do not receive your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photo library

The app uses your camera only to take your daily photo. If you save a timelapse, the app asks for permission to add the video to your photo library; it never reads your existing photos.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We don't use your photos to train AI models.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all my data to permanently delete your photos, skin check results, habit logs and quiz answers from your phone. Deleting the app also removes them. To delete the scan counts and error reports linked to your anonymous ID on our server, email auracheck.app@gmail.com and we will delete them. Subscriptions are managed and canceled in your Apple ID settings.

Age

Glowstreak is only for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Data retention and security

Scan counts are kept while you use the app; error reports are deleted after 90 days. Data sent to our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data on your phone is protected by your device's security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because most data stays on your device, you can view and delete it directly in the app. For anything stored on our server, contact auracheck.app@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
